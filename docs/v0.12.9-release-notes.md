# VoteKit v0.12.9

## Explicit IRV preference transfers

- Every excluded candidate now has a ballot-by-ballot transfer breakdown in
  the online round results and official PDF report.
- Each breakdown states how many ballots transferred to every continuing
  candidate and how many exhausted because they had no further preference.
- IRV CSV exports include the same transfer summary for independent review.
- Historical closed elections receive the additional detail whenever their
  results page, PDF or CSV is regenerated. Stored ballots and declared outcomes
  are unchanged.

## Verification

- A regression fixture reproduces the published 45-ballot voting-timelines
  example: 4 of 5 excluded ballots transfer to `3 weeks`, 1 exhausts; then 2 of
  8 transfer to `2 weeks` and 6 exhaust, for 7 exhausted ballots in total.
- Full application tests, lint, type checking, regression checks, production
  build and dependency audit pass.

This release changes result presentation and derived count detail only. It has
no database migration and does not alter ballots, winners or voter data.
