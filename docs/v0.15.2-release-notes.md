# VoteKit v0.15.2 - Delete unpublished setup drafts

- Returning Officers and Owners can use **Delete draft** on their own unpublished
  setup drafts in the Elections list.
- Confirmation names the draft and explains that deletion is permanent and its
  proofing link will stop working.
- Pending requests disable the action; failed requests show an inline error.
  Successful deletion removes the row and announces completion.
- Server-side role, ownership and CSRF checks remain enforced. Draft deletion and
  its audit entry are committed together.
- Published elections, including not-yet-open ones, do not receive this action.
  Returning Officers still cannot delete active or finalised elections.
- Archive and Owner takeover policies are unchanged. No existing drafts or
  elections are automatically deleted; no schema or dependency changes.

Verification: 147 automated tests plus the production-copy rehearsal passed;
lint, type-check, regression checks, production build and dependency audit passed
(zero vulnerabilities). Chromium acceptance covered cancellation, failure/retry,
successful deletion, invalidated proof links, unchanged other drafts/elections,
and desktop/320px/390px layouts. Browser testing used isolated fixtures only.
